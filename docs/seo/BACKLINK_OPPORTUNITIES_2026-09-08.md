**Paid backlinks:** Buying links intended to pass ranking credit, bulk low-quality directory links, automated link schemes, and excessive reciprocal exchanges conflict with Google's link-spam policies. Paying for real advertising or sponsorship is permitted when links are appropriately qualified with `rel="sponsored"` or `nofollow`. Therefore, skip “100 high-DA dofollow links,” private blog networks, paid ranking-link insertions, and mass guest-post packages. A local sponsorship can be worthwhile for audience access and leads; require clear placement, duration, audience evidence, disclosure, and a qualified link rather than a ranking guarantee. [Google's link-spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam).

## Earn editorial links with actual useful material

These are proposed acquisition approaches, not confirmed offers from publishers. The publisher controls whether to mention/link and how to qualify a link; never require reciprocal linking.

1. **Completed-project evidence:** Publish four to six real Dallas/Fort Worth case studies with owner-approved photos, approximate location, roof problem, chosen solution, actual materials, and dates. Ask the business owner to identify existing suppliers or commercial clients whose own project stories could legitimately cite that work. Remove street-level residential details unless approved.
2. **DFW homeowner resource:** Publish a concise storm preparation and inspection checklist with municipal and weather sources, original photos, and a clearly explained inspection process. It must be useful enough for an HOA or property manager to choose to reference. Do not mass-publish the same article on third-party blogs.
3. **Existing trade relationships:** Review genuine manufacturer/distributor profiles and active memberships before buying new ones. Fixing a stale link on a profile the company already earns is often the easiest relevant opportunity.
4. **Real community work:** If SkyGuard already supports a neighborhood event or nonprofit roofing project, document the work and check the organizer's recognition process. Treat donation-linked placements as sponsorships. Do not invent charitable projects to justify links.
5. **Expert commentary:** Offer the owner's actual expertise to local publications when there is a relevant story. Useful topics include evaluating roofing estimates, documenting visible storm damage, and understanding material choices. Publication and a link are never guaranteed.

Outreach is an external communication and has not been sent. Case studies need real job records and permission; the website team cannot reasonably assume those facts.

**Weeks 3–6:** Evaluate one genuine association membership; publish the first real project writeups and homeowner resource. The NTRCA application is a stronger roofing-specific candidate than purchasing a generic link package. The owner identifies actual partners before any outreach.

**Monthly:** Check listing accuracy, qualified referral leads, and search visibility. Use Search Console's links report as one input when access is available; this research did not access it or a backlink database.

For each opportunity record: publisher, existing/new profile, owner login holder, source URL, submitted website URL, submission date, verified live date, public phone/address status, cost/renewal, link treatment where inspectable, referral sessions, qualified leads, and next review date. Keep credentials in the business password manager, never in the repository.

A submission counts as **submitted**, not live. A backlink counts as **verified** only after the source page is publicly checked and the actual link points to SkyGuard. A profile with no crawlable website link still may be valuable for customer discovery, but must not be reported as a followed editorial backlink. No search-score or ranking increase is guaranteed by a fixed link quantity.
