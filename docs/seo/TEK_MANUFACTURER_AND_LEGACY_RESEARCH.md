## Primary technical evidence

### Current code report — strongest ratings/assembly source

[CCRR-0443 PDF, hosted by TEK](https://www.tekroofingsystems.com/pdf/2026-02-11-CCRR-0443.pdf), eight pages, revision February 20, 2026; renewal date February 28, 2027. Linked by TEK's [dealer technical hub](https://www.tekroofingsystems.com/dealer-tools/).

The report covers named Hurricane/High Country Shake, Shake PRO, Tile and TEK Shingle products. Section 4.3 supports UL 2218 Class 4. Class A depends on the Table 2 assembly: plywood, specified synthetic underlayment and GAF VersaShield; it is not a blanket claim about bare steel. Table 3 distinguishes panel and fastening configurations. Standard Shake includes direct-deck variants and wood-batten configurations with loose-laid foam wedges; Shake PRO has different direct-deck screw configurations. Wind design values are assembly pressures, not a universal roof wind-speed guarantee. Deck/framing design remains required. The report specifies minimum 3:12 slope and says its conditions govern conflicting manufacturer instructions. Local approval remains with the building official.

Implementation: cite this source rather than reproducing the large technical table on a consumer page. Avoid unqualified fire/wind numbers. Document the selected product and assembly in the estimate. The source identifies reports/tests; it does not authorize use of Intertek certification marks for SkyGuard.

### Current installation manuals — prefer over old November 2024 PDF

- [HURRICANE Metal Shake manual](https://www.tekroofingsystems.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/3-HURRICANE-Metal-Shake-User-Manual.pdf), 32 PDF pages. Printed pages 7–8 show batten installation and foam placement; printed pages 19–20 address attachments/uplift. It describes a 28-gauge coated steel panel and includes maintenance/care requirements. This supports discussing a batten-and-foam approach **for the appropriate Shake system**, not asserting every TEK panel uses that method. Professional assembly selection still must match the current report and local code.
- [HURRICANE Metal Shake PRO manual](https://www.tekroofingsystems.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/4-HURRICANE-Metal-Shake-Pro-User-Manual.pdf), 27 PDF pages. Keep this distinct from Standard Shake. The document has separate panel/edge/fastening details; do not reuse a Standard Shake construction specification for PRO based on the brand alone.

Useful for discussing a coordinated system of panels, edge/hip/ridge details, accessories, battens and foam. A components catalog does not establish which assembly was installed on a specific SkyGuard job.

### Warranty — product-specific, limited, and conditional

[TEK Lifetime Limited Warranty PDF](https://www.tekroofingsystems.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/TEK-Warranty.pdf), one page. Despite the path date, this is the document linked from the current technical hub. Its text names **HURRICANE Metal Shake and HURRICANE Metal Shake PRO**, with a 50-year period from installation. It covers specified material defects, uplift up to 140 mph, qualifying hail penetration/substrate cracking or splitting, and deterioration beyond defined normal weathering. It excludes multiple causes including improper installation/handling, some other roof work/contact, alterations and environmental conditions. Transfer requires specified information and proof of purchase mailed within 30 days of property ownership change. Warranty repair/replacement and conditions follow the actual document; this is not a universal promise to replace every roof or cover installation workmanship.

Implementation: “Review the manufacturer warranty for the selected TEK product, including its coverage, exclusions and transfer requirements.” Do not extend this Shake/PRO document to Tile/Shingle without the matching warranty. Do not advertise cosmetic hail immunity, guaranteed lifetime service, or unrestricted transferability.

### Marketing conflicts that should not be copied

- [TEK homepage](https://www.tekroofingsystems.com/) describes a foam insert added during installation; the [Shingle page](https://www.tekroofingsystems.com/metal-roofing/metal-shingle/) and [Tile page](https://www.tekroofingsystems.com/metal-roofing/metal-tile/) use factory-bonded language. The technical report explicitly describes loose-laid wedges in certain assemblies. Do not assert factory bonding across all products. Use “foam insert, where specified for the selected system.”
- Some pages combine “CAT-5” marketing with 140-mph wording. Use neither as a universal performance promise; consult the applicable pressure/assembly documentation for the actual project.
- The Shingle page makes no-maintenance/no-fading/indefinite-foam claims. Installation manuals include care and maintenance instructions. Avoid “maintenance-free,” “never replace,” and guaranteed cost/energy payback.
- No verified installed-roof R-value, household energy saving percentage, rain-noise reduction figure, or property resale premium was established here. Do not infer those from generic foam properties or marketing comparisons.
- [TDI impact-resistant roofing guidance](https://www.tdi.texas.gov/company/roofing-discounts.html) says each insurer sets accepted tests, labels, documentation and discounts. Ask the insurer about eligibility for the selected material; do not promise a discount or present TDI as a product endorsement.
