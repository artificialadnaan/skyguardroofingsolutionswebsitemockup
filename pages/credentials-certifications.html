Understand the credential behind the badge

Manufacturer programs relate to particular roofing products and program requirements. Chamber memberships connect a business with its local business community. A referral program helps homeowners find independent professionals. Each has a different purpose; use the details below alongside a written estimate, references and current project documents.

TAMKO Pro Gold Certified Contractor

SkyGuard is a TAMKO Pro Gold Certified Contractor. Gold is a specific level within TAMKO’s contractor program, with product education and access to qualifying warranty options. It is useful when discussing a TAMKO shingle roof and the components required for the proposed system.

TAMKO’s program information identifies Gold eligibility for the TAMKO Shield enhanced limited system warranty. Eligibility depends on the actual roof system and written terms. TAMKO Shield does not include workmanship coverage. Ask which warranty is included in your proposal and what registration is required.

Open TAMKO’s general contractor locator or contact the manufacturer to check current status and eligibility.

CertainTeed ShingleMaster

SkyGuard holds the CertainTeed ShingleMaster credential. CertainTeed’s roofing credential programs cover product and installation knowledge, giving homeowners a useful starting point for a discussion about shingles, roof-system components and installation requirements.

Our designation is ShingleMaster. When comparing proposals, match the exact credential and selected roof system to the warranty being offered. Ask us to identify the applicable product documents and written coverage before you choose a CertainTeed roof.

Read CertainTeed’s contractor-program information and contact CertainTeed to confirm the designation relevant to your project.

Fort Worth Chamber member

SkyGuard is a member of the Fort Worth Chamber. Our public directory profile gives homeowners an independent place to identify the business and find its contact information. Membership reflects a local business connection; it is separate from a manufacturer’s roofing credential.

View SkyGuard’s Fort Worth Chamber profile. For roof-planning questions specific to the city, our Fort Worth roofing page also links permit and historic-property resources.

Azle Area Chamber member

SkyGuard is also a member of the Azle Area Chamber of Commerce. Homeowners can use the Chamber’s public listing to check the business name, contact details and website when researching a contractor in the wider Fort Worth area.

View SkyGuard’s Azle Chamber profile. Tell us your property location when requesting an inspection so we can discuss access and the scope of work.

Home Depot Pro Referral participant

SkyGuard participates in Pro Referral, powered by The Home Depot. The program connects customers with independent service professionals. Home Depot’s Pro Referral screening explanation describes a third-party background check of the business owner and a general liability insurance requirement for participating pro businesses.

These are program requirements, not a check of every worker assigned to a property. Confirm the people responsible for your work, current insurance evidence and written project terms with us before authorizing a job.

Verify the details for your roof

The Chamber links above open SkyGuard’s own public profiles. The manufacturer and Pro Referral links open general program resources. For manufacturer verification, provide our full business name and Fort Worth location and ask the program administrator to confirm the current designation and project eligibility.

Which warranty will my project receive?

Ask for the exact warranty document, qualifying products, registration steps, exclusions and transfer terms. Manufacturer coverage and contractor workmanship terms should be identified separately in your roof replacement proposal.

What insurance evidence should I request?

Request a current certificate of insurance and confirm the named business, covered work and relevant dates with the insurance contact. Discuss the coverage needed for your property and scope; a program badge does not describe every project’s insurance protection.

How do these credentials affect material selection?
