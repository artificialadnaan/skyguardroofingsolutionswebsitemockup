Fence Installation & Repair in DFW

Explore fence repair and replacement scopes, material options, gates, and property considerations in Dallas–Fort Worth.

Plan the fence line before choosing materials

SkyGuard discusses fencing projects for homes and commercial properties. Share the desired purpose, approximate length, existing fence condition, and gate requirements. Confirm the property line and any easements before committing to the layout; a fence location is not a substitute for a survey.

What we discuss

Materials and appearance

Discuss wood, vinyl, metal, or other proposed systems, including height, finish, maintenance, and compatibility with the site.

Gates and access

Identify pedestrian and vehicle openings, hardware, direction of travel, and daily access needs.

Site conditions

Review removal, grading, drainage, vegetation, utilities, and any existing damaged posts or footings.

What belongs in a fence scope?

Specify the layout, materials, post and gate details, demolition, disposal, and any finishing work. Check local and HOA requirements and arrange applicable utility-location steps before excavation. Changes to the layout or hidden site conditions should be documented before extra work.
