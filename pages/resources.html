Dallas–Fort Worth Roofing Resources

Official local permit, storm, and insurance resources to help DFW homeowners plan roofing work and evaluate a written estimate.

Start with the right local authority

Use the property’s actual jurisdiction when checking requirements. A roof covering replacement, structural repair, commercial project, or historic property may follow different processes. Confirm the current requirements before work begins.

  • Dallas residential permits: permit forms, remodel guidance, and city contacts.
  • Dallas Landmark review: exterior work and Certificate of Appropriateness procedures.
  • Fort Worth residential building permits: applications, remodel requirements, and assistance.
  • Grand Prairie residential re-roofing: city-specific permit and contractor registration requirements.

Storm and insurance information

  • National Weather Service Fort Worth/Dallas: forecasts, hazard information, radar, and local weather resources.
  • Texas Department of Insurance: roofing and insurance: contractor roles, insurance decisions, and deductible rules.

Use official alerts for immediate weather information. A weather report can provide context, but it does not establish damage or insurance coverage for an individual property.

Roof maintenance checklist for DFW homeowners

  • From the ground, look for new stains, displaced roofing, blocked drainage and debris after severe weather.
  • Keep a record of roof age, previous repairs, inspections and product documents.
  • Arrange safe gutter and drainage maintenance; leave roof access and work at height to equipped professionals.
  • Ask an inspector to review flashings, penetrations and visible deterioration when a leak or change appears.
  • Follow the specific roofing manufacturer’s maintenance requirements and keep records of completed work.

Questions to ask before hiring a roofer

  • What condition was observed, and why is repair or replacement recommended?
  • Which materials and flashing details are included in the written scope?
  • Who handles applicable permits, inspections, and required approvals?
  • How are concealed damage, changes, cleanup, and payment documented?
  • Can you review current insurance evidence, references, and any claimed manufacturer credential?
  • What do the actual product and workmanship warranty documents cover?
