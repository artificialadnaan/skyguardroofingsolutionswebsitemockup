After a storm: safety, documentation, then a repair plan

Wait until the weather has passed and the property is safe. Stay off the roof, avoid fallen power lines, and keep clear of unstable ceilings or structural damage. Check current alerts from the National Weather Service Fort Worth/Dallas office.

From a safe location, record when the problem appeared and photograph visible damage. Save receipts for reasonable temporary protection and contact your insurer with policy questions. If water is entering the building, call SkyGuard to discuss access and availability; scheduling depends on conditions and demand.

What a roof assessment can help clarify

Observed condition

Review accessible roof materials, flashing, penetrations, and nearby damage. Photos record observations; a weather report alone does not prove damage to a particular roof.

Repair scope

Identify a targeted repair or explain why a larger replacement assessment is appropriate. Scope and concealed conditions are discussed before work is authorized.

Useful records

Keep dated photographs, written construction estimates, contracts, change approvals, invoices, and product information together for your own records.

Roofing work and insurance decisions

SkyGuard can document roof conditions and prepare a construction estimate. Your insurer determines coverage and payment under the policy. We do not promise a claim approval, negotiate a settlement on your behalf, or waive your deductible.

The Texas Department of Insurance explains roofing and insurance rules, including the restriction on a contractor acting as a public adjuster on work the contractor may perform. See our roofing documentation guide for a practical checklist.

Questions before you schedule

Do I need a replacement after every hailstorm?

No. A storm does not establish the condition of every roof. An inspection helps identify visible damage and the appropriate construction scope.

Can I inspect the roof myself?

Document what is visible from a safe location. Leave roof access and work at height to appropriately equipped professionals.

Can you guarantee same-day storm service?

No. Call to discuss the situation and current availability. Weather, safe access, crew scheduling, and storm demand affect response times.

Hail damage questions for Fort Worth property owners

Did last night’s hail damage my roof?

A report of hail in Tarrant County does not tell you what happened at your address. From the ground, note new leaks, displaced material or visible damage to gutters and other exterior surfaces. Those observations justify a closer assessment; they do not establish the amount of roof damage. Avoid climbing onto the roof to check.

What does hail damage look like?

Its appearance depends on the roof material. Surface marks, granule loss and dents need to be distinguished from wear, installation issues or earlier damage. An assessment should document observations on the actual roof and explain whether repair, monitoring or replacement evaluation is appropriate.

Do I need to file an insurance claim?

Ask your insurer about your policy, reporting requirements and deductible. Keep dated photographs and the roofing assessment for your records. SkyGuard prepares construction documentation; your insurer determines coverage.

Would a Class 4 roof prevent another hail loss?

An impact-test classification is not a guarantee against hail damage. Discuss the exact product’s rating, installed assembly and warranty, and ask your insurer about any eligibility requirements. Our stone-coated steel roofing guide explains product-specific questions for a material upgrade.
