What is stone-coated steel roofing?

Stone-coated steel combines formed steel panels with a stone-granule finish. TEK offers shake, tile and shingle profiles, giving homeowners a choice of shapes rather than the raised vertical seams associated with standing seam. Review the TEK product and technical resources alongside physical samples; a screen cannot show every texture or color variation.

For your proposal, identify the exact product name, profile and color. Different TEK panels can have different installation requirements. The selected panel should be documented consistently in the estimate, technical information and warranty records.

Battens, direct-to-deck installation and foam

There is no single installation method for every stone-coated steel roof. The Intertek CCRR-0443 evaluation report includes both batten-and-foam configurations and direct-to-deck configurations for listed standard Hurricane Metal Shake panels. Other panels and assemblies have their own requirements; the method for one TEK product should not be assumed suitable for another.

Our scope discussion covers the existing deck, underlayment, attachment, valleys, wall connections, penetrations and roof edges. Ask which approved assembly is proposed and how its details will be followed. The TEK Hurricane Metal Shake installation manual provides product-specific installation guidance; it is not a substitute for assessing your roof.

Foam used in some configurations can provide panel support. That is a separate question from whole-roof insulation or household energy savings. Confirm whether foam is part of the selected assembly and what documentation supports any thermal claim. We do not assume a particular R-value or promise lower utility bills from the panel choice alone.

Impact ratings and Fort Worth storm exposure

Section 4.3 of CCRR-0443 states that the metal panels recognized in the report meet UL 2218 Class 4 impact resistance. Match that finding to the proposed product and required assembly. A laboratory impact classification is not a promise that a roof will be hail-proof, free of cosmetic damage or covered for every storm loss.

Stone-coated steel roofing questions

How long will a stone-coated steel roof last?

There is no reliable lifespan promise for every roof. The selected product, installation, exposure, maintenance and actual condition matter. Review the written product information and care requirements; a warranty period is not a prediction of the roof’s service life.

Will my insurer pay for a stone-coated steel upgrade?

Ask your insurer whether your policy covers the loss and how an upgraded material would be treated. SkyGuard can document roof conditions and provide construction estimates. Coverage decisions belong to the insurer; review the Texas Department of Insurance roofing guidance before discussing claim terms.

Does every TEK roof use foam or battens?

No. Requirements depend on the specific panel and approved assembly. Ask to see the applicable installation detail and confirm the role of any foam. Panel support does not establish a whole-roof energy-performance claim.

Can stone-coated steel go over my existing roof?

Do not assume an overlay is appropriate. The existing assembly, deck condition, product instructions and applicable code must be reviewed first. The estimate should state whether tear-off is included and how concealed damage will be handled.

Is impact resistance the same as an insurance discount?

No. An insurer may have its own eligibility and documentation requirements. Confirm the exact product, installation evidence and policy terms directly with the insurer before counting on a discount. A Class 4 rating does not eliminate storm risk.
