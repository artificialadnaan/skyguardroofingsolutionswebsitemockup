Roofing & Home Improvement Financing

Plan your roofing budget, compare written project scopes, and ask SkyGuard about current third-party financing options for DFW projects.

Start with the construction estimate

Get a written scope for the roof repair, replacement, or property improvement before deciding how to fund it. Ask which work is included, what could change if concealed damage is found, and when payments are due. A clear scope helps you compare the total project cost.

Contact SkyGuard to confirm current third-party financing availability for your project. Financing eligibility, rates, fees, terms, and funding timing are determined by the provider. An inquiry or roofing estimate does not guarantee credit approval.

Compare the full cost of an offer

  • Annual percentage rate (APR), fees, and the total amount repayable.
  • Monthly payment, repayment term, and any prepayment conditions.
  • Eligibility, the application process, and when a credit check may occur.
  • Whether the product is intended for a homeowner or business borrower.
  • How funding timing fits the construction payment schedule.

Acorn Finance publishes information about its financing marketplace. You can review its current information directly or ask our office about available options. Do not send credit applications or financial records through our website contact form.
