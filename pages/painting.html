Interior & Exterior Painting in DFW

Discuss surface preparation, paint selection, and a defined interior or exterior painting scope for your Dallas–Fort Worth property.

A useful paint estimate begins with the surfaces

SkyGuard provides painting scope discussions for homes and commercial properties. Tell us which rooms, walls, trim, cabinetry, or exterior areas are involved and whether you have noticed peeling, staining, or moisture. Address the source of water entry before covering a stain.

What we discuss

Surface preparation

Identify cleaning, patching, sanding, caulking, priming, and protection of nearby surfaces in the written scope.

Product and finish

Agree on the product, colors, sheen, and the areas to be coated. Confirm compatibility with the existing surface and expected maintenance.

Access and scheduling

Discuss occupied rooms, furniture, pets, ventilation, weather constraints for exterior work, and the cleanup plan.

Before the first coat

A proposal should define the surfaces, preparation, coating system, exclusions, and treatment of extra repairs. Ask about existing coatings and any property conditions that require specialized evaluation before disturbance. Do not treat a fresh coat as a repair for an unresolved roof or wall leak.
