Commercial Roofing in Dallas–Fort Worth

Roof assessments, repair scopes, replacement planning, and maintenance discussions for DFW commercial buildings.

Start with the building and the roof system

Commercial roofing decisions affect occupants, equipment, drainage, and business operations. SkyGuard works with property owners and managers to evaluate roof concerns and define the next construction step. Tell us the building use, approximate roof area, access requirements, and whether leaks are disrupting occupied space.

We can discuss low-slope systems such as TPO and EPDM, metal roofing, and the suitability of repair or replacement for the existing assembly. System choice depends on the substrate, drainage, equipment, installation requirements, and the scope confirmed for the property.

Priorities for a commercial assessment

Leaks and roof details

Review accessible seams, flashing transitions, penetrations, roof edges, and the areas associated with reported leaks.

Drainage and equipment

Discuss drains, scuppers, ponding concerns, rooftop units, service access, and coordination with other trades.

Occupancy and scheduling

Identify tenant access, delivery routes, staging, noise-sensitive periods, and a workable construction sequence.

Repair, replacement, or planned maintenance?

A localized defect may be suitable for roof repair. Widespread deterioration or an unsuitable assembly may require replacement planning. A maintenance discussion can identify observations to document and the next inspection interval appropriate to the roof and its warranty requirements.

Request a written scope specifying the system, preparation, flashing, drainage work, permits, and any manufacturer inspection or warranty requirements. Do not assume an unspecified warranty or installer certification applies; confirm the actual documents for the selected system.

Questions before you schedule

Can work be coordinated around business hours?

We can discuss occupancy and access constraints when developing the schedule. Timing depends on the approved scope, weather, materials, and safe working conditions.

Can a coating solve every flat-roof leak?

No. Suitability depends on the existing system, condition, moisture, drainage, and product requirements. An assessment is needed before recommending a coating or other repair.

What should property managers send first?

The address, building use, roof type if known, leak locations, access rules, and any available roof plans, repair history, or warranty documents.

Discuss your commercial roof

Tell us your city, the roof problem, and how to reach you. We will follow up to discuss access and scheduling.
