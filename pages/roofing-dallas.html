Roofing in Dallas, TX

Dallas roof repair, replacement, and storm damage assessments from SkyGuard Roofing Solutions, based in Fort Worth and serving the DFW area.

Plan roofing work for your Dallas property

Whether you are tracing a recurring leak or planning a full replacement, begin with the roof condition and the property address. SkyGuard serves Dallas from our Fort Worth base. Share your neighborhood, roof type, access restrictions, and whether there is an active leak so we can discuss the right assessment and appointment.

For a localized issue, review roof repair. For broader deterioration or a planned upgrade, explore roof replacement. Commercial owners can request an assessment that accounts for rooftop equipment, drainage, and tenant access through commercial roofing.

Dallas permits and historic property review

Confirm the permit requirements for the actual scope with Dallas Permitting & Inspections before work begins. The city uses DallasNow for its permitting services; the Dallas residential permits page provides remodel forms and contact information. A mailing address alone does not establish the permitting jurisdiction.

Dallas Landmark properties and properties in Landmark Districts require a Certificate of Appropriateness before exterior work begins. Even a same-color shingle replacement may need routine-maintenance review. Check the city’s Landmark Review Process before ordering materials or scheduling work. HOA approval, where applicable, is a separate check.

Bring these details to your estimate

  • Any historic designation, HOA material restrictions, or prior city correspondence.
  • Roof age if known, previous repair records, and locations of interior stains.
  • Parking, alley or driveway access, gates, pets, and landscape protection needs.
  • The proposed material, ventilation and flashing details, and how hidden decking damage will be handled.

After hail or wind, our storm damage service can help document visible conditions and prepare a construction scope. Your insurer makes coverage decisions.

Questions before you schedule

Does SkyGuard have a Dallas office?

Our published office is in Fort Worth. Dallas is a service area; contact us to confirm scheduling for your address.

How do I know whether historic review applies?

Use the city’s preservation resources and confirm the property status with Dallas staff before exterior work. The required review depends on the property and proposed changes.

What should I do about an active Dallas roof leak?

Keep clear of unsafe areas and call (682) 330-5088 to discuss availability. Do not climb onto a wet or damaged roof.
