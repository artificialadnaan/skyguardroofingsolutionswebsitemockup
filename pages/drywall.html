Drywall Installation & Repair in DFW

Discuss drywall repair, new installation, texture matching, and project preparation for Dallas–Fort Worth homes and businesses.

Address the cause before repairing the wall

SkyGuard discusses drywall work for damage, remodeling, and new interior construction. For water stains, soft material, or recurring cracking, establish whether roof, plumbing, moisture, or structural concerns need evaluation first. A cosmetic patch should not hide an unresolved problem.

What we discuss

Repair work

Review the affected area, damaged material removal, patching, taping, and the finish expected for the repaired surface.

New installation

Coordinate the proposed board type and assembly requirements with the project plans, room use, and any applicable fire or moisture considerations.

Finish coordination

Discuss texture, primer, paint, dust control, protection of occupied areas, and what level of blending is practical with an existing finish.

Define the finished result

The written scope should identify the rooms and surfaces, installation or repair details, finish, cleanup, and painting inclusions or exclusions. Existing textures and lighting can affect the appearance of a patch; discuss expectations before work begins.
