Mesquite, TX Hailstorm Recovery: Complete Roof Repair Guide

Published June 1, 2026 • By SkyGuard Roofing Solutions

Editorial review: September 8, 2026

A Mesquite hailstorm can leave a homeowner making several decisions at once: whether the roof is leaking, who should inspect it, whether to contact insurance, and what can wait. Separate urgent protection from the choice of a permanent roof repair.

Record what changed

Wait until conditions are safe, then photograph visible missing shingles, dents, broken exterior materials, and interior water stains from safe positions. Include wide views as well as details so the location is clear. Do not climb onto the roof, even to collect a loose shingle. A storm’s reported hail size or a neighbor’s replacement does not prove the same damage occurred on your roof.

Request an evidence-based assessment

Have the roofer identify the affected areas and explain the observations supporting repair or replacement. Ask whether aging, installation issues, or previous repairs also contribute. A useful report separates what was inspected from what could not be accessed and identifies any need for further investigation. It should not promise that a particular finding will produce a full insurance payment.

Keep temporary work separate

If roofing is exposed or water is entering, ask a qualified contractor about temporary protection and current availability. Approve the scope and charge in writing and retain before-and-after documentation. A temporary cover is not a replacement roof; agree on inspection and follow-up needs. Stay out of rooms with electrical hazards, unstable ceilings, or suspected structural damage.

Handle the claim with your insurer

Contact your insurer promptly about reporting requirements, deductibles, and covered work. SkyGuard’s role is inspection, photographs, and construction estimates; we do not represent homeowners on coverage or negotiate their settlements. Ask the insurer how to submit additional construction documentation when hidden damage is discovered. Texas prohibits a contractor from waiving or absorbing your deductible.

Select materials using actual documentation

If replacement is appropriate, compare specific shingle products and their published impact ratings, wind requirements, and warranty terms. An impact rating describes a test result; it is not a guarantee against every hailstorm. Ask your insurer whether an exact product qualifies for a discount and obtain the amount in writing before budgeting any savings.

Close out the project

Keep the agreed scope, approved changes, photos, permit and inspection records where applicable, invoices, and warranty documents together. Confirm who handles follow-up if a leak appears. Schedules depend on material availability, weather, approvals, and contractor capacity; ask for current dates rather than assuming a standard six-to-ten-week process.
