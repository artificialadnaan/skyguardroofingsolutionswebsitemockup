Siding Installation & Repair in DFW

Discuss siding replacement, storm-related repairs, and exterior details for your Dallas–Fort Worth home.

Plan the wall system, not just the finish

Siding work should account for the condition behind the panels, transitions around openings, flashing, trim, and the selected product’s installation requirements. SkyGuard discusses replacement and repair scopes for existing homes and new construction in the DFW area.

What we discuss

Material choices

Compare vinyl, fiber cement, engineered wood, or other proposed materials for the project. Ask about appearance, installation details, maintenance, and the written product warranty.

Damaged areas

Describe loose panels, visible deterioration, moisture concerns, or storm damage. The condition behind existing siding may need further evaluation when materials are removed.

Scope and coordination

Identify trim, soffit, fascia, paint, disposal, and repairs to underlying materials as included items or explicit exclusions.

Before authorizing siding work

Confirm the proposed product, color, required approvals, and treatment of windows and doors. Ask how concealed damage is priced and approved. Where roof-to-wall transitions are involved, coordinate the flashing detail with the roofing scope.
