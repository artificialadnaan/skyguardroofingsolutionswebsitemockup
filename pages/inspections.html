Roof Inspections in Dallas–Fort Worth

Request a roof inspection for your DFW home or business. Discuss visible conditions, repair options, and next steps with SkyGuard.

Know what needs attention

A roof inspection helps turn a leak, storm concern, or aging roof into a practical next step. Tell us what you have noticed and whether the issue is active. We review the accessible areas relevant to the concern, explain observations, and discuss whether a repair or replacement assessment is appropriate.

Inspection access depends on the building, weather, and safety conditions. Concealed components cannot be fully evaluated visually; additional investigation or work may be needed. Ask the team to confirm the scope and no-cost inspection eligibility when scheduling.

What we discuss

Visible roof condition

Roof covering, flashing, penetrations, roof edges, drainage concerns, and signs of damage in accessible areas.

Property history

Roof age if known, earlier repairs, interior stains, storm timing, and any existing product or project records.

Next steps

Observed concerns, photographs where available, repair or replacement options, and the scope of any further investigation.

Request your roof inspection

Share your contact details and property city. We will contact you to confirm scope, eligibility for a no-cost inspection, access, and scheduling. This request does not reserve an appointment.

We use the details you submit to respond to this request. Do not include policy numbers, payment details, or other sensitive records.
